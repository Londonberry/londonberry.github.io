## The problem

Most high school coaches scout opponents by hand, or don't scout at all. There's no time between practice, travel, and everything else.

## What we built

Blackbook makes advance-scout reports a coach can print and carry into the dugout: hitter breakdowns, spray charts, and recent at-bats for the team they play next.

- **The PDF is the product.** The website is a quick preview and a checkout.
- **AI-assisted, scout-reviewed.** AI does the heavy lifting on the data, and a person reviews every report before it goes out.

## Results

- **75 paid orders**, most of them during the May and June playoffs.
- **18 of 34 coaches bought again.**
